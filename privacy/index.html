Privacy policy

Your privacy

This policy explains how Soft Bulb LLC, operating as Soft Bulb Studios, handles information connected with this website, our games, and direct communications.

Effective September 26, 2026

Information you provide

This website does not use a public account system or contact form. If you email us, we receive the email address, message, and any files or information you choose to include. We use that information to respond to your request and provide relevant support or services.

Website and service data

When you choose light or dark mode, we store that preference in your browser so it can be remembered across pages and visits. This preference is not sent to us. You can remove it by clearing this website's browser storage.

Our hosting and infrastructure providers may process limited technical information, such as an IP address, browser type, device information, and request logs, to deliver the website, maintain reliability, and protect against abuse.

Games and third-party platforms

Our games may be distributed through third-party platforms such as Steam, Apple's App Store, or Google Play. Those platforms process information under their own privacy policies. If a game collects additional information, we will provide game-specific disclosures before or within that product.

Consulting engagements

Information shared for consulting work is handled for the purposes described in the relevant project agreement. Client data is not sold or used to advertise unrelated products.

Retention and sharing

We keep information only as long as reasonably needed for support, business, security, and legal purposes. We do not sell personal information. We may share limited information with service providers that help us operate, or when required to comply with law and protect rights, safety, or security.

Children

This website is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review and remove it where appropriate.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as our products and services change. The effective date above identifies the current version. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to the address below.
