Game & product support

How can we help?

For technical issues, gameplay questions, account concerns, or general feedback, email our support inbox.

games@softbulb.io↗

What to include

Tell us the game or product name, the device or platform you are using, and what happened. Screenshots and the steps that led to the issue are helpful when available. Please do not send passwords, payment information, or other sensitive personal information.

The Fourth Sense Evolution

For help with The Fourth Sense Evolution, include your operating system and game version if you know them. You can also visit the game's Steam page for current product information.
