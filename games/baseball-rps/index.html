About the game

Read the pitch.
Play your hand.

Baseball RPS turns each at-bat into a card matchup. Choose your pitcher or batter, anticipate your opponent’s rock, paper, or scissors, and account for the wind.

Player traits add another layer to every decision. Weigh their strengths and drawbacks to build a ballclub that works together.

How it plays ↓Build your team ↓

Inside a standard at-bat

A hidden pitch.
A changing wind.
Your next move.

01

Set the pitch

The defending side plays a pitcher card face-down. The batting side knows a pitch is coming, but not which card.

02

Read the wind

The wind reveals which rock-paper-scissors type it will support. Use that information while weighing the hidden pitch.

03

Choose your batter

Play a position-player card from your hand. Consider its matchup, traits, and the runners already on base.

04

See the result

The pitcher’s card is revealed. The matchup, wind, and player traits resolve the at-bat as the action moves around the bases.

Build your ballclub

The strategy starts
before the first pitch.

Build a roster of up to 26 players under a salary cap. Arrange your batting order and pitching rotation, then manage substitutions as the game unfolds.

01 / Roster

Make room for your best

Choose how to spend your salary budget across the players in your ballclub.

02 / Traits

Find the right combinations

Weigh each player’s strengths and drawbacks, and how they complement the rest of the team.

03 / Lineup

Think a few turns ahead

Your batting order and pitching rotation shape the cards you draw and the decisions you can make.
