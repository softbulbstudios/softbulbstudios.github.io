Baseball RPS

Privacy policy

This policy explains what information Baseball RPS handles, why, and the choices you have. Baseball RPS is made by Soft Bulb LLC. It adds to our general privacy policy.

Effective November 20, 2026

In short

Baseball RPS shows no ads and does not use your device’s advertising ID. We do not sell your information or share it for advertising. You can delete your account and its data at any time.

Your account

The first time you play online, the game creates a player account for you automatically. It has a player number and no email address or password. You can link it to Sign in with Apple on iPhone and iPad, or to Google Play Games on Android, or move it to another device with a transfer code, so you can get it back later. When you link it, we receive an account identifier from Apple or Google. We never receive your password.

Information the game stores

Names you choose. Your manager name, your team name and the country flag you pick.

Your game data. Saved progress, card collection, decks, in-game currency and items, quests, rank and leaderboard entry.

Match records. The cards and plays of the games you play. Our servers use them to referee matches and to show results and replays.

Friends and chat, if you use them. Your friends list, the players you block, chat messages between friends, and reports you send. Chat is limited to friends and passes through a filter that masks offensive words and links.

Feedback, if you send it. The text you write in Settings › Send feedback.

Purchases, if you make them. What was bought and when. Payment is handled by the App Store or Google Play. We never see your card details.

Diagnostics and technical data

The game engine sends crash reports, diagnostics and performance data, together with a device identifier and device details such as model and operating system version, so we can find and fix problems. Our servers and service providers also process technical information such as IP address and request logs to run the game and protect it against abuse.

What the game does not do

It shows no ads and contains no advertising or analytics trackers. It does not ask for your contacts, location, photos, camera or microphone. Reminders are scheduled on your own device and are not sent through us.

What other players can see

Other players can see your manager name, team name, flag, rank and your plays in matches against them. Friends can see the chat messages you send them.

Who processes it

We use Unity Technologies to run player accounts, cloud saves, game servers and diagnostics. Apple and Google provide the stores, sign-in and purchases under their own privacy policies. An email provider delivers feedback and support mail to us. These providers handle information only to provide their services. We may also disclose information when required to comply with law or to protect rights, safety or security.

Keeping and deleting

We keep your account and game data for as long as the account exists. You can delete the account and its data in the game or by email at any time. The steps, and what is erased and what is kept, are on the account deletion page.

Children

Baseball RPS is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us so we can remove it.

Your choices and rights

You can ask for a copy of your information, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local law. Email us at the address below and we will respond.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the game changes. The effective date above identifies the current version. Questions and privacy requests: rps@softbulb.io.
