Baseball RPS

Delete your account

You can delete your Baseball RPS account and the game data stored with it at any time, in the game or by email. Baseball RPS is made by Soft Bulb LLC.

In the game

On the title screen, tap your manager portrait at the top to open Your Club, then tap Delete account and confirm. You need to be online. Your account and its data are erased right away. If the connection drops part-way, the game says so — tap Delete account again to finish.

By email

If you can no longer open the game, email rps@softbulb.io with the subject “Account deletion” and your manager and team names. We confirm the request and delete the account and its data within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your player account and the game data stored with it: your manager and team names, country, saved progress, card collection, decks, in-game currency and items, your friends list and chat messages, and your leaderboard entry. Your side of your recent matches and any game in progress — your name, squad and plays — is erased from those match records.

What is kept

The opponent’s half of a match you played stays with the match, without your details. Older match records may still contain an anonymous player number that no longer links to any account. Purchases are processed by Google Play or the App Store, which keep their own records under their policies.
